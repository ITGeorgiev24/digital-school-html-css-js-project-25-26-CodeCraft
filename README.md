<h2 id="about-the-project">About The Project</h2>
    
<p align="justify">
QuizMaster is a browser-based quiz platform where users can test their knowledge across categories like Science, Geography, History, and more. Players earn XP and level up from Rookie to Grandmaster by  answering questions correctly, with a full leaderboard to compete against others. The app includes a quiz generator, achievements,  personal stats, and detailed history so you can track your progress over time. An admin panel lets moderators manage users, custom quizzes,  and platform content.
</p>
